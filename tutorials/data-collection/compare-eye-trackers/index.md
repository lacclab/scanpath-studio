# Compare two eye trackers

Choosing between two eye trackers, or two recording setups, for an experiment? Record the same text with each and compare the scanpaths.

1. [Load](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-collection/check-a-session/#1-load-the-session) each recording as its own dataset, each with the **Recording setup** of the screen it was recorded on.
1. Open the first tracker's dataset and select a trial.
1. Turn on **Compare**. In scanpath B's row, choose the second tracker's dataset under **Dataset B**, then a trial of the same text — trials of the same text are marked 📄.
1. Compare the two readings: precision, how fixations sit on the words, missing stretches. Recordings on screens of the same size can be overlaid; otherwise each is drawn on its own screen, side by side. Turn on **Animate** to replay both on one clock.
