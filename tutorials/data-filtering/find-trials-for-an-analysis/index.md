# Find the trials an analysis needs

Locate trials containing particular fixation patterns.

1. Open Plot controls → Filters & highlights → Fixations.

1. Set the relevant category to Highlight:

   - Short or Long, with a duration threshold in milliseconds
   - Outside the text, for fixations outside every word box
   - Near a blink, if your data includes a blink column

1. Step through the trials. In Annotations, tag those that meet your analysis criteria, for example `include`.

1. Open the filter button beside Select trial. Under By trial annotation, choose `include` in With any of these tags.

1. [Check and export the selection](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-filtering/#every-task-ends-the-same-way).
