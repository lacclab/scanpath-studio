# Analyze one text

How was one text read — which words drew long fixations, and how do its readers differ?

1. [Define the pool](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/corpus-analysis/#before-you-start), then open **Corpus Analysis → Per text** and pick the text.
1. Choose one familiar measure: total fixation duration for overall attention, first-pass reading time for initial processing, or regression rate for rereading.
1. Read the word profile on the stimulus, or against a word property such as surprisal. Set a minimum number of participants per word, so isolated observations do not appear as stable estimates.
1. Check how many participants contribute, and open a scanpath view to investigate surprising words.
