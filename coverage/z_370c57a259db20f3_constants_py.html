205# --- VIZ-18 · selectable palettes -------------------------------------------- 

206# These figures don't only get looked at on the screen they were made on: they go 

207# into papers (printed, sometimes in black & white) and are read by colourblind 

208# viewers. One palette can't serve all of that, so the colour defaults are a 

209# *choice* rather than a constant. 

210# 

211# A palette is a preset, not a second rendering path: picking one writes the 

212# ordinary per-element colour keys, so every existing picker still overrides it 

213# and every surface (deep link, Save & restore, CLI, API) carries the resulting 

214# colours with no new plumbing. ``palette_settings`` returns the figure-kwarg 

215# form; ``controls.apply_palette`` writes the session keys. 

216# 

217# Rules each non-default palette follows: 

218# * hues distinguishable under deuteranopia/protanopia (no red-vs-green pair 

219# carrying meaning on its own), and 

220# * **lightness** ordered as well as hue, so the figure still reads after a 

221# greyscale conversion. Marker shape (VIZ-15) and the two-way saccade mode 

222# (VIZ-19) are the redundant channels when colour alone can't carry it.

294def palette_settings(name: str) -> dict: 

295 """Figure-kwarg colour settings for palette ``name`` (falls back to Default). 

296 

297 Returns a fresh dict (nested ``saccade_class_colors`` copied too), so callers 

298 can mutate the result without corrupting the registry. 

299 """ 

300 entry = PALETTES.get(name) or PALETTES[DEFAULT_PALETTE] 

301 settings = {k: v for k, v in entry.items() if k != "description"} 

302 settings["saccade_class_colors"] = dict(settings["saccade_class_colors"]) 

303 return settings
