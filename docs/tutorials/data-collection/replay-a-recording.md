# Replay a recording

A still scanpath hides timing. Turn on **Animate** to replay the reading
fixation by fixation, and look for:

- long missing periods;
- repeated points out of bounds;
- frequent interruptions, such as a participant blinking often;
- a vertical shift that grows during the trial, a sign of calibration drift.

To make these stand out, open **:material/filter_list: Filters & highlights →
:material/blur_on: Fixations** in the plot rail and **Highlight** short, long or
out-of-bounds fixations. Highlighting keeps the whole trial visible; keep
**Discard** for a later, documented filtering decision.
