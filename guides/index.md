# Feature guides

Each guide explains one part of the app, with Python and CLI examples where they apply. For step-by-step workflows, see the [tutorials](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/index.md).

- **[Scanpath visualization](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/guides/scanpath-visualization/index.md)**

  Layers, filters, replay, and comparison, also across two datasets.

- **[Data Management](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/guides/loading-data/index.md)**

  Built-in and public datasets, importing your files, column mapping and the recording setup.

- **[Corpus Analysis](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/guides/corpus-workspace/index.md)**

  Per text, Per participant and Groups, from the reading measures in your data.

- **[Export and share](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/guides/exporting/index.md)**

  Figure downloads, share links and code, batch export and replay export.
