# Analyze one text

Compare how participants read the words of one text.

1. [Select the trials](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/corpus-analysis/#before-you-start), then open **Corpus Analysis** → **Per text**.
1. Pick a text and a reading measure your data provides.
1. In **View**, choose **Measure on the stimulus**. To plot the measure against a word feature such as surprisal, choose **Measure vs feature** and pick the **Feature**. Your Words table must have that column.
1. In **Aggregate**, choose how the values are combined. **Cohort profile** also draws a **Spread** band, and its **Min participants per word** leaves out words that too few participants have a value for.
1. [Download the table and recipe](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/corpus-analysis/#keep-the-result).
