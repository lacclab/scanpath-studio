# Tutorials

Each tutorial walks one research task from start to finish. New here? Start with [Install](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/install/index.md).

- **[Optimize data collection](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-collection/index.md)**

  Pilot your eye tracker, software, and stimulus layout (font, spacing) before you commit — for example, [compare two eye trackers on the same text](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-collection/compare-eye-trackers/index.md). Catch calibration and setup problems that only show up in a plot, and train new experimenters to spot issues such as frequent blinking, or check their sessions as you supervise.

- **[Validate your data](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-collection/check-a-session/index.md)**

  Mark trials or whole sessions for exclusion, and decide whether you need to collect more. Check that fixation detection and line assignment hold up on this dataset by viewing fixations against the raw gaze and word boxes.

- **[Filter data for analysis or publication](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-filtering/index.md)**

  Exclude or flag poorly calibrated, skimming, or non-reading trials, and trials with out-of-bounds, very short, or very long fixations. Annotate trials as you look through them: star them, tag them (such as `not reading`) or add notes, then filter by those tags. Find the trials a specific analysis needs — for example, participants with a given native language and good calibration — and export the marked trial IDs.

- **[Export customized figures](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/exporting-figures/index.md)**

  Show a real scanpath in a paper or talk as a heatmap or a replay, and save the look as a design to reuse. Or bulk-export every trial consistently, for example as training data for models that generate scanpaths on images.

- **[Compare and analyze](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/corpus-analysis/index.md)**

  Compare model-generated scanpaths with human ones, contrast groups and conditions, and see how reading progresses over time.

The app also has short interactive tutorials under **Help → Tutorials**. They highlight each control as you go, and never change your data or settings.
