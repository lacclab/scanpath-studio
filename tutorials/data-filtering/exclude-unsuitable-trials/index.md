# Exclude unsuitable trials

Apply your exclusion criteria and leave rejected trials out of the selection.

1. [Review the session](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-collection/check-a-session/#2-inspect-a-trial). Judge trials against your study's criteria; a scanpath pattern alone does not establish skimming or poor calibration.
1. In Annotations, tag rejected trials `exclude` and add a brief reason.
1. Open the filter button beside Select trial. Under By trial annotation, enter `exclude` in Excluding tags.
1. [Check and export the selection](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-filtering/#every-task-ends-the-same-way).

For trials with several screens, apply the exclusion tag to the whole trial. A screen-only annotation does not exclude its parent trial.
