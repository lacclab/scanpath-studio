# Compare groups or conditions

Do two populations or conditions read differently?

1. [Define the pool](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/corpus-analysis/#before-you-start), then open **Corpus Analysis → Groups**.
1. Define one cohort — split a field, or build a filter set — and check that it looks right.
1. Turn on comparison, define the second cohort, and read **Group means & difference**. It compares the two cohorts' per-participant means and is descriptive: there is no significance test.
1. Read it with its denominator: the caption says how many participants are in each cohort and how many are in both. A participant in both contributes to both means, and then the standardized difference is not shown — see [how it is computed](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/computations/#agg-effect-size).
