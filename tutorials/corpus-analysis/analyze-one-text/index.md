# Analyze one text

1. [Select the trials](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/corpus-analysis/#before-you-start), then open **Corpus Analysis** and choose a **Reading measure**. In the bundled demo, try **Total fixation duration — TFD**.
1. In **Texts**, select a dot or use **Find a text**, then choose **Open word profile**. The **Cohort profile** shows a point for each word with enough observations. Hover over a point to read the word and its value.
1. Open **Readings behind a word** to check which participants supplied a value. A blank cell means that reading had no recorded value for the selected measure. In **Chart settings**, **Aggregate** sets how the profile combines values, **Spread** controls the shaded band, and **Min participants per word** sets how many readers must contribute.
1. In **Chart settings**, use **View** to explore the same text. **Measure on the stimulus** colors the words in their original positions. **Measure vs feature** compares the reading measure with a feature in the Words table, such as surprisal.
1. [Download the table and recipe](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/corpus-analysis/#keep-the-result).

If your data has no mapped **Text ID**, open **Texts** to inspect a single reading by its trial ID. To compare several readings, map the Words column that identifies the same passage across participants. If the page asks for reading measures, **Map reading measures** opens the column mapping for an editable dataset. For an upload that contains only fixations or gaze samples, **Add a Words table** opens the editor where you can attach the report.
