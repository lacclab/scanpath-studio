# Exclude unsuitable trials

Apply your exclusion criteria and leave rejected trials out of the selection.

These steps use the **Full** mode: switch to it in the top bar of the **Scanpath** view, or add `ui=full` to the app's address ([Essentials and Full](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/guides/scanpath-visualization/#essentials-and-full)).

1. [Review the session](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-collection/check-a-session/#2-inspect-a-trial). Judge each trial against your study's criteria. A scanpath pattern alone does not establish skimming or poor calibration.
1. In **Annotations**, tag rejected trials `exclude` and write a short reason in **Notes**.
1. Open the filter () beside **Select trial**. Under **By trial annotation**, add `exclude` to **Excluding tags**.
1. [Check and export the selection](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-filtering/#every-task-ends-the-same-way).

For a trial with several screens, tag the whole trial. A tag on one screen does not exclude the trial.
