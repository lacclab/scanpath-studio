# Find the trials an analysis needs

Some analyses need particular trials: ones with very short or very long fixations, fixations out of bounds, or many blinks.

## 1. Make them stand out

Open **Filters & highlights → Fixations** in the plot rail and **Highlight** the category you are after: **Short** or **Long** (each with its own threshold in ms), **Out of bounds** (fixations in no word box), or **Blink** (fixations at or next to a blink — it needs a blink column in your data).

## 2. Bring them to the top

Use the ⇅ trial-ordering popover beside the trial picker to sort the trials, for example by reading time to surface unusually short or long ones, and step through them with ◀ ▶.

## 3. Mark them

Tag the trials the analysis needs in **Annotations**, then keep only them: in the filter funnel under **By trial annotation**, put the tag in **With any of these tags**.

**Then:** [check and export the pool](https://lacclab.github.io/scanpath-studio/tutorials/data-filtering/#every-task-ends-the-same-way).
